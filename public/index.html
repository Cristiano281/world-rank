Heritage Watch

A timeless mechanical design.

Heritage Watch was born from a simple idea: bring back the feeling of a bygone era, when watches were made slowly, by hand, and built to be kept for a lifetime. Every detail — the open-heart dial, the gold-finished case, the domed sapphire crystal — was chosen to echo the golden age of watchmaking, when craftsmanship came before speed.

Each piece is made with genuine care, the way collector's items always are: not rushed, not mass-produced in spirit, even when made in numbers. Two versions carry this same soul forward — a mesh-strap edition designed for her, and a leather-strap edition designed for him.

This isn't just a watch. It's meant to be the kind of object that earns its place in a drawer for decades, then gets passed on to someone else who'll understand why it mattered.

Heritage Watch is currently in pre-launch. Your donation doesn't reserve a specific unit — it helps bring this design to life, and adds your name to the waitlist to be notified first when it launches.

This is a donation to support development, not a pre-order — it does not guarantee a unit or a delivery date.
